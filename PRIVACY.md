# Privacy Policy

**Vello** (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what data the Vello macOS app may collect and how it is used.

*Last updated: August 2, 2026*

## Summary

- Screen recordings and exported files stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them.
- The native app does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not show advertising in the app.

## Data stored on your device

Vello stores preferences (for example recording options, keyboard shortcuts, and your chosen save folder) locally with `UserDefaults` inside the app sandbox. Recordings are written to temporary space while you edit them; exports go to a folder you choose through the save panel (or a folder you previously granted via a security-scoped bookmark).

## Permissions

Vello may ask for:

- **Screen Recording** — required to capture your screen
- **Microphone** — only if you enable audio recording

These permissions are managed by macOS. You can revoke them in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Network use

Core recording and export do not require a network connection.
